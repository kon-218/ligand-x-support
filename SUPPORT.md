# Support policy

This repository is the canonical public support and issue-tracking location for all Ligand-X product
components.

## Public issues

Use an issue for reproducible product defects, installation failures, scientific correctness concerns,
feature requests, and documentation corrections. Keep one actionable problem per issue. Public issues
may be searched, referenced in release notes, and retained as part of the product's engineering record.

## Discussions

Use [Discussions](https://github.com/kon-218/ligand-x-support/discussions) for usage questions,
workflow advice, and open-ended ideas. A maintainer may convert a discussion into an issue once it has
a specific, actionable outcome.

## Private support

Email [support@ligand-x.com](mailto:support@ligand-x.com) for account access, billing, commercial
licensing, licence keys, confidential datasets, or any matter that should not be public. Do not email
ordinary bug reports merely to avoid the public tracker; a public issue makes status and resolution
visible to all affected users.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the normal support process.

## Service expectations

Maintainers review reports as capacity permits. Labels and milestones communicate current state, but
they are not service-level guarantees. Paid support terms, if applicable, are governed by the relevant
customer agreement rather than this public policy.
