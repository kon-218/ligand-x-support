# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Ligand-X Inc. is committed to a professional, respectful, and inclusive support community. Be precise
and constructive, assume good faith, respect differing experience levels, and criticize ideas or
results rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, publishing another person's private information, sexualized content, or repeated unwanted
contact.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct undermines a safe and productive community. Decisions
will consider context, impact, and recurrence.

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@ligand-x.com](mailto:support@ligand-x.com). Reports will
be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. This code applies in repository spaces and when
someone officially represents Ligand-X Inc.
