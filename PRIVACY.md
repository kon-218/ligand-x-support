# Privacy and sensitive-data guidance

GitHub issues and Discussions in this repository are public. Search engines and third-party archives
may retain content even after it is edited or deleted.

Before posting logs, screenshots, configuration, structures, trajectories, or result files, remove:

- passwords, API tokens, licence keys, cookies, and registry credentials;
- names, email addresses, home directories, hostnames, IP addresses, and internal URLs;
- proprietary molecular structures, compound identifiers, unpublished results, and customer data;
- protected health information or other regulated personal data; and
- commercial terms, invoices, and account details.

Use a small public structure or synthetic example whenever possible. GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting is not a general confidential file-transfer mechanism. Contact
[support@ligand-x.com](mailto:support@ligand-x.com) before sharing sensitive reproduction material.

If you accidentally publish a secret, revoke or rotate it immediately, then contact support and delete
the content. Deletion from the visible issue does not guarantee removal from forks, notifications,
caches, or archives.
