# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do **not** open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability or include exploit details in Discussions.
Use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/kon-218/ligand-x-support/security/advisories/new).
If GitHub is unavailable or the report concerns sensitive account context, email
[support@ligand-x.com](mailto:support@ligand-x.com) with the subject
`Security report: <short description>`.

Include, where safe:

- the affected Ligand-X component and version;
- the platform and deployment configuration;
- reproduction steps or a proof of concept;
- the potential impact;
- whether the issue is already public; and
- a safe way to contact you.

Do not send real licence keys, access tokens, customer datasets, or regulated data. We will acknowledge
the report, investigate it, coordinate remediation and disclosure when appropriate, and credit
reporters who request attribution. Ligand-X Inc. will not pursue good-faith security research that
respects privacy, avoids service disruption, and provides reasonable time to remediate before public
disclosure.

## Scope

Reports may cover the launcher, distributed runtime images, gateway and web interface, update and
licensing mechanisms, product website, or other Ligand-X-operated services. Vulnerabilities in an
unmodified third-party dependency should also be reported to that project's security contact; tell us
when a Ligand-X distribution is affected.
